Decisions shown here — approve or change

  1. The menu bar is icon-only: a dot means work in progress, orange means something needs you, a small triangle means something was left behind. Text there gets hidden when the menu bar is full (taking the icon with it); the detail lives in the panel and notifications.
  2. Order in the panel: needs-you first (oldest waiting), then in progress by most recent activity, parked last.
  3. Needs you = a waiting gate, an agent asking for input, or closed but not merged. Colour: orange icon and badge; its text uses a darker orange that stays readable on a light panel.
  4. A repo that can't be read keeps its last good data. Its rows say stale rather than fading, so their text and buttons stay readable; only the dots dim. Parked builds work the same way.
  5. Left behind never takes over the menu bar: just a small ⚠ beside the icon. It isn't urgent.
  6. Fast-lane builds show 4 dots (Phases 1, 5, 6, 7); full lane shows 7. The waiting gate's dot is orange.
  7. Cleanup is copy-only. Chief Stew never runs it.
  8. Notifications only for needs-you; a reminder every 30 min while a gate waits (configurable). Never for progress.
